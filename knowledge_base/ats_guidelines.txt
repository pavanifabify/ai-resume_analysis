ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Resume Guidelines

What is an ATS
An Applicant Tracking System is software that companies use to collect, scan, and rank
resumes before a human recruiter ever sees them. A large share of mid-size and large
companies use some form of ATS. If your resume is not ATS-friendly, it may never reach
a human reviewer, regardless of how qualified you are.

Formatting Rules for ATS Compatibility
1. Use a single-column layout. Multi-column layouts, text boxes, and tables often get
   scrambled or skipped entirely by ATS parsers.
2. Use standard section headings: "Experience", "Education", "Skills", "Projects".
   Creative headings like "My Journey" or "What I Bring" may not be recognized.
3. Save as .pdf (text-based, not scanned) or .docx unless the application specifies
   otherwise. Avoid image-based PDFs, as ATS cannot read text embedded in images.
4. Avoid headers/footers for critical information (name, contact info) - some ATS
   systems do not parse header/footer regions.
5. Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman) at 10-12pt. Avoid decorative
   fonts.
6. Avoid graphics, icons, and charts to represent skill levels - ATS cannot interpret
   a visual "skill bar" and it wastes space that could hold parseable keywords.
7. Spell out acronyms at least once (e.g., "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)") so both
   ATS keyword matching and human reviewers understand you.

Keyword Optimization
- Mirror the exact terminology used in the job description where truthful. If the JD
  says "React.js" and you know React, write "React.js", not just "front-end framework".
- Include both the acronym and full term for key technologies (e.g., "Machine Learning
  (ML)").
- Place your most relevant skills and keywords near the top third of the resume, since
  some ATS ranking algorithms weight earlier content more heavily.
- Do not keyword-stuff invisible white text or hidden characters to game the ATS - many
  modern systems flag this and it looks dishonest if a human ever inspects the file.

Common ATS Rejection Reasons
- Contact information not in the body text (e.g., only in a text box or image).
- Non-standard date formats that confuse the parser (use "Jan 2023 - Present" or
  "01/2023 - Present" consistently).
- Missing an explicit skills section, forcing the ATS to infer skills from prose.
- File corruption or password protection preventing the file from opening at all.

ATS Compatibility Score Factors
When evaluating ATS compatibility, consider: single-column layout, standard section
headers, appropriate file format, absence of tables/images for critical content,
keyword alignment with target roles, consistent date formatting, and a clear,
parseable skills section.
